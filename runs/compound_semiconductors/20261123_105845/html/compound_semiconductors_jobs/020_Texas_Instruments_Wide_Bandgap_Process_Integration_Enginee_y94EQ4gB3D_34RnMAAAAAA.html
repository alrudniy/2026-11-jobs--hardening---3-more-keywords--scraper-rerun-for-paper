Job description

Originally posted 28 October 2026 by the employer.

About the role

This position is focused on the development, characterization, and optimization of gallium nitride (GaN) power devices within Texas Instruments' Advanced Technology Development (ATD) team. The role supports developing and scaling GaN power process technologies for high-volume manufacturing in collaboration with fab/manufacturing, ...business units, and Kilby Labs.

What you'll do

- Perform GaN device simulations (TCAD), device design, and layout. - Develop robust process flows, drive design-of-experiment (DOE) execution, and solve complex integration challenges. - Collaborate with fab/manufacturing engineers to ensure device manufacturability. - Collaborate with reliability engineers to meet device reliability requirements. - Conduct failure root-cause investigations for device, process, reliability, and yield improvement.

What you'll need

- PhD in Electrical Engineering, Electrical & Computer Engineering, Materials Engineering, Physics, or related degree. - Research experience in GaN or compound semiconductor device/process development. - Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0. - Understanding of Power FETs, semiconductor device physics, and GaN high electron mobility transistor (HEMT) process flows. - Hands-on experience with electrical and materials characterization methodologies and statistical data analysis.

Nice to have

- Familiarity with GaN-on-Si technology for power switching applications. - Experience with fab tools and process modules/Integration. - Knowledge of device physics, reliability mechanisms, and packaging interaction.

Skills: GaN, HEMT, TCAD, process integration, wide bandgap

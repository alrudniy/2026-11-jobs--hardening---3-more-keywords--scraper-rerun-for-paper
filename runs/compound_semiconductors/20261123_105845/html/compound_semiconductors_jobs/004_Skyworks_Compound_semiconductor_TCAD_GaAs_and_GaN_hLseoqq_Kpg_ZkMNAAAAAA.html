Job description

Support the modeling, simulation, and development of advanced compound semiconductor epitaxial structures using TCAD tools, with a focus on GaAs and GaN technologies.

About the role

As a Winter/Spring Co-op Intern, you will support the modeling, simulation, and development of advanced compound semiconductor epitaxial structures using TCAD tools, with a focus on GaAs and GaN technologies.

What y...ou'll do
• Design and evaluate new epitaxial structures through ML/AI Driven Epi Development
• Define machine learning models for epitaxial development
• Setup/automate TCAD simulations for model training
• Investigate optimization routines for ML training
• Validate optimized epitaxial structures through device test

What you'll need
• Currently enrolled in a PhD graduate program, studying Electrical Engineering, Material Science, Chemical Engineering, or Computer Science
• Knowledge in Device Simulation Platforms [Sentaurus, Silvaco]
• Knowledge in ML/AI models

Nice to have
• Strong Computer Programming skills [Python]
• Understanding of Compound Semiconductor (GaAs and/or GaN based devices) stack-ups and their influence on device performance
• Basic understanding of device fabrication process steps, yield analysis and DC/RF device parameters

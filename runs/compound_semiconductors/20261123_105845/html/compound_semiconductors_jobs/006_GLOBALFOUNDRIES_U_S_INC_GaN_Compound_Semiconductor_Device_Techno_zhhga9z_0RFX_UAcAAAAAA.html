About GlobalFoundries GlobalFoundries (GF) is a leading manufacturer of essential semiconductors, enabling AI at scale from the cloud to the physical world. Through deep partnerships with customers, GF delivers differentiated, power efficient and high‑performance solutions for high‑growth markets. GF is a trusted and holistic technology partner for customers around the world. GF’s talented, global... team remains focused every day on security, longevity, and sustainability. For more information, visit www.gf.com. New College Graduates Overview: We offer many full-time employment paths for recent graduates, which provide accelerated training in a fast-paced work environment, cross-functional working opportunities, and talent mobility. New college graduates are provided with mentorship, networking, and leadership opportunities, which give our new team members life-long connections and skills. Summary of Role: GF Labs is seeking a highly motivated New College Graduate PhD to develop next-generation compound semiconductor technologies for power, RF, and emerging high-performance applications, with emphasis on relationships among materials, device architecture, fabrication, reliability, and system performance. This broad device-technology position may contribute to high-frequency power conversion, integrated power delivery, RF applications, and extreme-voltage or extreme-environment electronics, depending on technical background and evolving GF roadmap priorities. Essential Responsibilities include: Research and evaluate next-generation GaN, AlGaN, and wide-bandgap semiconductor devices. Model and experimentally characterize electrical, thermal, and reliability behavior. Investigate new lateral and vertical device architectures for power and RF applications. Understand and optimize relationships among epitaxy, materials, processing, device architecture, and electrical performance. Analyze mechanisms affecting breakdown, dynamic performance, trapping, leakage, reliability, and thermal behavior. Develop experimental test structures and characterization methodologies for emerging device concepts. Collaborate across materials, device, process integration, modeling, circuit, and packaging teams. Translate application and system requirements into semiconductor device requirements and technology opportunities. Evaluate emerging semiconductor materials and architectures for future GF technology platforms. Collaborate with universities, research institutes, suppliers, and industry partners, and communicate recommendations to multidisciplinary teams and leadership. Other Responsibilities: Perform all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and programs.

About the role

This Senior MTS Process Integration Engineer will drive materials and device technology development for vertical GaN technology. This role offers high technical ownership and cross-functional visibility, shaping onsemi’s power device roadmap.

What you'll do
• Deliver multiple aspects of materials and device technology developme...nt from substrate through to singulated die.
• Partner with the process engineering team to develop new processes, optimize process windows, ensure device robustness, and resolve integration challenges.
• Design and analyze experiments to improve device performance, yield, and reliability.
• Drive structured root-cause analysis for yield, reliability, or device-related issues, and implement sustainable corrective solutions.
• Present technical content in executive-level reviews and represent the team in cross-functional discussions.

What you'll need
• M.S. in Materials Science, Electrical Engineering, Physics, or a related field.
• 10+ years of experience in compound semiconductor technology development and/or manufacturing.
• Understanding of gallium nitride crystal structure and crystalline defects.
• Demonstrated ability to translate high-level objectives into actionable engineering plans and drive projects independently.
• Hands-on data-driven problem-solving skills using DOE, statistical analysis, SPC, 6-sigma, and FMEA methodologies.
• Track record of leading or significantly contributing to semiconductor materials and device technology development projects with measurable impact on performance, reliability, yield, or cost.
• Experience taking technologies from development into early manufacturing.

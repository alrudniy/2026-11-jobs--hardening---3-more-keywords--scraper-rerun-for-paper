Job description

Tesla AI Hardware Team Position

The Tesla AI Hardware team is at the forefront of revolutionizing artificial intelligence through cutting-edge hardware innovation. Comprising brilliant engineers and visionaries, the team designs and develops advanced AI inference chips tailored to accelerate Tesla's machine learning capabilities. A key part of this effort is Dojo, Tesla's custom supercomputer sys...tem built to efficiently train massive neural networks on vast video data from the fleet. The work of Tesla's AI Hardware team powers the neural networks behind Full Self-Driving (FSD), and Tesla humanoid robot, Optimus, pushing the boundaries of computational efficiency and performance. By creating custom silicon and optimized architectures, the team ensures Tesla remains a leader in AI-driven automotive and energy solutions, shaping a future where intelligent machines enhance human life.

Candidate in this role will apply their academic knowledge to enable successful launch of complex SOC's starting from first silicon bring up, characterization, qualification through volume production.

We are open to hiring in Austin, TX; Palo Alto, CA.

Responsibilities
• Initial silicon bring-up with variety of targeted tests - structural, functional, parametric
• Characterize various IP's, processors cores on across process, voltage, temperature
• Evaluate silicon life using accelerated stress, debug failures, quantify performance shifts
• Analyze data from volume production, identify solutions to improve yield, throughput
• Use different platforms such as ATE, SLT, Bench for silicon evaluation

Requirements
• Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related fields, or equivalent experience
• Solid understanding in CMOS fabrication process
• Solid understanding of digital, analog, mixed signal circuits
• Good understanding of DFT concepts such as ATPG, MBIST is a plus
• Statistical data analysis techniques, automation skills

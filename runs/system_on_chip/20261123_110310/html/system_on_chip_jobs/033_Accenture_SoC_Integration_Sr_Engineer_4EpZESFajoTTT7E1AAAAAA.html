Job description

Accenture is looking for a highly motivated and experienced SoC Integration Sr. Engineer to join our passionate and innovative team. We are looking for a self-starter who can manage multiple projects and demands with a positive, team-oriented attitude.The ideal candidate for this position will have a deep understanding of system-on-chip (SoC) integration and related technologies, with proven exper...ience in the design, development, and implementation of these systems. The successful candidate should have a broad knowledge base, excellent problem-solving and communication skills, and the ability to collaborate effectively with a wide range of stakeholders.We are looking for someone with a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, and a minimum of 5 years of experience in SoC integration. Additionally, prior experience in software development, hardware design, and FPGA/ASIC design, as well as expertise in scripting and simulation tools, is highly desirable.

Responsibilities:
Design, develop, and implement SoC integration systems.
Manage multiple projects and demands with a positive, team-oriented attitude.
Monitor SoC integration technologies and trends.
Collaborate effectively with a wide range of stakeholders.
Create and maintain software development, hardware design, and FPGA/ASIC designs.
Utilize scripting and simulation tools.
Troubleshoot and debug system-on-chip integration issues.
Evaluate existing systems and recommend improvements.
Prepare technical documents and presentations.
Provide training and support to team members.

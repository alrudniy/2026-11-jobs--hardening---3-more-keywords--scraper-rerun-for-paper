Job description

QBit Semiconductor | Littleton, MA | Full-Time | Hybrid

About QBit Semiconductor

QBit Semiconductor develops advanced System-on-Chip (SoC) solutions for smart imaging, artificial intelligence, and intelligent edge devices. Our technology combines high-performance image processing, always-aware low-power operation, and mixed-signal integration to deliver highly integrated, cost-effective semicond...uctor solutions.

At QBit, engineers have the opportunity to work on sophisticated SoC designs and contribute directly to products from initial architecture and RTL development through silicon implementation and validation.

We are looking for an experienced ASIC / SoC Design Engineer to join our engineering team in Littleton, Massachusetts.

Position Overview

The primary responsibility of this position is the design and development of digital logic at the RTL level. You will participate in the complete SoC development process, including architectural definition, microarchitecture, RTL implementation, simulation, synthesis, integration, and silicon validation.

This is a hands-on engineering role offering exposure to a broad range of SoC technologies and the opportunity to work closely with architecture, design verification, physical design, and silicon validation teams.

Depending on experience and interests, design responsibilities may include:
• Image processing and computer vision accelerators.
• Memory controllers, cache architectures, and memory interfaces.
• DMA engines and high-performance data movement.
• AMBA AXI/AHB interconnects, bridges, and system controllers.
• Standard peripheral and high-speed interface controllers.
• Low-power digital design and clock/reset management.

Key Responsibilities
• Develop and implement digital designs using Verilog and SystemVerilog.
• Participate in architectural definition, microarchitecture, and detailed design specifications.
• Develop, simulate, and debug RTL designs at the block and subsystem levels.
• Collaborate with design verification engineers to ensure functional correctness and verification coverage.
• Support SoC integration, including lint, clock-domain crossing (CDC), and logic synthesis.
• Work with physical design engineers to resolve timing, area, power, and implementation issues.
• Review timing constraints and static timing analysis (STA) results and assist with timing closure.
• Support gate-level simulation and post-silicon debugging and validation.
• Work with third-party IP providers and internal engineering teams to integrate complex SoC subsystems.
• Contribute to design quality, engineering best practices, and successful project execution.

Required Qualifications
• BSEE or MSEE in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
• Solid understanding of digital design fundamentals, synchronous logic, state machines, pipelining, and clock/reset architectures.
• Experience with RTL simulation
• Familiarity with the ASIC implementation flow, including synthesis, timing closure, and gate-level simulation.
• Experience with industry-standard EDA tools, preferably Synopsys or Cadence.
• Ability to debug complex design issues and work effectively across engineering disciplines.
• Strong communication and interpersonal skills, including the ability to collaborate with globally distributed engineering teams.

Preferred Qualifications
• 5+ years of professional experience in ASIC or SoC digital design.
• Strong proficiency in Verilog and SystemVerilog RTL development.
• Experience in logic synthesis, timing constraints, and static timing analysis.
• Experience with SoC architecture and subsystem integration.
• Familiarity with AMBA AXI/AHB protocols and interconnect architectures.
• Experience with memory controllers, DDR/LPDDR interfaces, or high-speed peripheral interfaces.
• Knowledge of low-power design techniques and clock/power management.
• Experience with design verification methodologies, including SystemVerilog/UVM.
• Familiarity with CDC analysis, RTL lint, and formal verification.
• Programming or scripting experience using Python, Tcl, C/C++, or shell scripting.
• Experience with post-silicon bring-up, lab debugging, and silicon validation.

Why Join QBit?

At QBit, you will be part of a highly experienced engineering organization developing sophisticated semiconductor products.

Unlike highly specialized roles at larger companies, our engineers have the opportunity to work across multiple aspects of SoC development, take ownership of meaningful design challenges, and see their work progress from RTL to working silicon.

We offer a collaborative engineering environment where technical contributions matter and engineers have direct involvement in product development.

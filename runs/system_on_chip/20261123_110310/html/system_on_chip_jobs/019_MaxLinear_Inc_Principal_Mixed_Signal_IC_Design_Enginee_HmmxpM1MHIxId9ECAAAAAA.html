Job description

Responsibilities

MaxLinear is seeking a Principal Mixed-Signal IC Design Engineer to join our Analog Mixed-Signal (AMS) group. In this role, you will serve as a technical lead for mixed-signal SoC products across our interface and connectivity portfolio, taking them from specification through production. You will focus on the following:
• Own the mixed-signal architecture and top-level design of ...complex SoCs, from product definition and specification through tapeout, qualification, and production release
• Lead in-house development and/or third-party integration of high-speed interface and PHY IP, including architecture definition, IP selection, vendor evaluation, PHY-to-controller interfacing, and top-level analog/digital partitioning
• Design and supervise high-speed analog front-end circuits such as transmit drivers, receivers, equalizers, clock and data recovery (CDR), and serializer/deserializer circuits
• Lead design of clock generation and distribution circuits such as PLLs, oscillators, and clock drivers to meet system-level jitter and phase-noise requirements
• Design and supervise supporting analog blocks including LDOs, bandgap references, POR/reset generation, bias circuits, comparators, and I/O and ESD structures
• Drive top-level chip integration, power and clock planning, floorplanning, pad ring, and package co-design with layout, digital, and package teams
• Define and review mixed-signal verification strategy with digital verification teams, including behavioral models, AMS co-simulation, and top-level checks
• Drive IP make/reuse/buy decisions and provide technical input on schedule, effort, and resource planning for new product programs
• Mentor and guide senior and junior designers; conduct design reviews and provide regular updates through documentation and presentations
• Lead post-silicon bring-up, debug, and characterization, and support test, product, applications, and operations teams through qualification and production ramp
• Implement design methodologies that meet company and industry standards for product reliability, yield, and industry-standard compliance

Qualifications
• Proven track record of leading mixed-signal IC products from specification to production, with multiple successful tapeouts in advanced CMOS processes
• Strong technical expertise in mixed-signal and analog IC design, with the ability to make sound architectural trade-offs across analog, digital, and system boundaries
• Experience in one or more of the following areas is highly desirable
• PLLs, DLLs, oscillators, and clock distribution; jitter and phase-noise analysis
• High-speed serial interface and PHY design and integration (e.g., PCIe, USB, Ethernet, MIPI, or other SerDes-based standards), including TX/RX, equalization, and CDR circuits
• Interface and connectivity SoCs with embedded controllers, and related standards compliance testing
• Analog building blocks (LDOs, bandgap references, POR, comparators, oscillators)
• I/O, ESD protection schemes, latch-up, and signal/power integrity considerations
• Mixed-signal verification methodology, including Verilog-A/Verilog-AMS behavioral modeling
• Good understanding of analog layout, device matching, parasitics, and process/reliability effects in deep-submicron CMOS
• Hands-on experience with lab bring-up and characterization using oscilloscopes, phase-noise/jitter analyzers, and protocol analyzers
• Demonstrated technical leadership, mentoring ability, and strong written and verbal communication skills
• BS in Electrical Engineering or related + 9 years of experience, or MS + 7 years of experience, or Ph.D. + 4 years of experience

Company Overview

MaxLinear is a global, NASDAQ-traded company (MXL) where the entrepreneurial spirit is alive and well. We are a fabless system-on-chip product company, striving to improve the world’s communication networks for everyone through our highly integrated radio-frequency (RF), analog, digital, and mixed-signal semiconductor solutions for access and connectivity, wired and wireless infrastructure, and industrial and multi-market applications.

We hire the best people in the industry and engage them in some of the most exciting opportunities that connect the world we live in today. Our growth has come from innovative, bold approaches to solving some of the world’s most challenging communication technology problems in the most efficient and effective manner.

MaxLinear began by developing the world’s first high-performance TV tuner chip using standard CMOS process technology. Others said we couldn’t achieve the extremely high-performance requirements using CMOS, but we proved them wrong and achieved enduring global market leadership with our designs. Since then, we’ve developed a full line of products that drive 4G and 5G infrastructure; enable data center, metro and long-haul optical interconnects; bring 10Gbit to the home; power the IoT revolution; and enable robust and reliable communication in harsh industrial environments. Over the years, we’ve expanded through organic growth and through several acquisitions that have perfectly complemented our existing portfolio and enabled us to deliver complete end-to-end solutions in our target markets. One such example was the acquisition of Intel’s Home Gateway Platform Division that added Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and Broadband Gateway Processor SoC technology to our connected home portfolio creating a complete and scalable platform of connectivity and access solutions to fully address our customers’ needs.

Our headquarters are in Carlsbad, near San Diego, California. We also have major design centers in Irvine and San Jose, California; Valencia, Spain; Bangalore, India; Munich, Germany; Israel; and Singapore.

We have approximately 1,200 employees, a substantial majority of whom have engineering degrees and include masters and Ph.D. graduates from many of the premiere universities around the world. Our employees thrive on innovation, outstanding execution, outside-the-box thinking, nimbleness, and collaboration. Together, we form a high-energy business team that is focused on building the best and most innovative products on the market.

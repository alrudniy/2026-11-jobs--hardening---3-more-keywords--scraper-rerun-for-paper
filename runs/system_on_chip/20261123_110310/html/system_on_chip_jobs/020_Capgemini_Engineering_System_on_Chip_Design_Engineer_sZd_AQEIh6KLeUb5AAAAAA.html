About The Job You're Considering

We are seeking a talented SoC Design Engineer to play a critical role in the architecture, integration, and implementation of complex System-on-Chip (SoC) solutions. In this role, you will take ownership of SoC subsystems throughout the design lifecycle, from architectural definition and IP integration through implementation support and silicon readiness. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams including design, verification, physical implementation, and PCB engineering to deliver high-performance, power-efficient solutions. This is an exciting opportunity for engineers who are passionate about advanced semiconductor design and enjoy solving complex technical challenges in a highly collaborative environment.

Your Role
• Define and document architecture specifications for SoC subsystems and contribute to overall chip architecture decisions.
• Integrate complex subsystems at the SoC level, including processors, memory, and high-speed interfaces.
• Collaborate with IP vendors and internal stakeholders to select and configure optimal IP solutions.
• Develop and maintain timing, power, and quality methodologies, including SDC constraints, UPF implementation, CDC/RDC analysis, and linting flows.
• Analyze power, performance, and area results and drive design optimizations to meet product objectives.
• Partner with synthesis, DFT, and physical design teams to resolve timing, power, and implementation challenges.
• Support PCB design and system integration activities to ensure successful product development and deployment

Your Skills & Experience
• Master's degree in electrical engineering or a related technical discipline, or equivalent industry experience.
• Four+ years of experience in SoC integration, design, or related semiconductor engineering roles.
• Strong experience integrating complex SoC subsystems such as processors, PCIe, DDR, or similar technologies.
• Proficiency with RTL design methodologies and hardware description languages including Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL.
• Experience developing and maintaining CDC, RDC, linting, synthesis, and power analysis flows.
• Strong scripting and automation skills using languages such as Tcl and Python.
• Excellent verbal and written communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively across global engineering teams.

Preferred Qualifications
• Experience integrating ARM Cortex-A or Cortex-R class processors.
• Experience integrating GPU-based subsystems and graphics architectures.
• Knowledge of MMU-based systems and cache-coherent architectures.
• Experience with RTL-to-GDS implementation flows.
• Familiarity with verification methodologies such as UVM.
• Experience using Synopsys tool suites including VCS, Verdi, SpyGlass, Design Compiler, and RTL Architect.
• Working knowledge of embedded software development using C.

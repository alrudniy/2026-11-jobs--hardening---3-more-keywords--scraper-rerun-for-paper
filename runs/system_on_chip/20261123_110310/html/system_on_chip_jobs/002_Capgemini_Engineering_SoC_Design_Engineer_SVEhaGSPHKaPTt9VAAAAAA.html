Responsibilities

  • In this role, you will take ownership of SoC subsystems throughout the design lifecycle, from architectural definition and IP integration through implementation support and silicon readiness
  • You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams including design, verification, physical implementation, and PCB engineering to deliver high-performance, power-efficient solutions
  • This is an exciting opportunity for engineers who are passionate about advanced semiconductor design and enjoy solving complex technical challenges in a highly collaborative environment
  • Your RoleDefine and document architecture specifications for SoC subsystems and contribute to overall chip architecture decisions
  • Integrate complex subsystems at the SoC level, including processors, memory, and high-speed interfaces
  • Collaborate with IP vendors and internal stakeholders to select and configure optimal IP solutions
  • Develop and maintain timing, power, and quality methodologies, including SDC constraints, UPF implementation, CDC/RDC analysis, and linting flows
  • Analyze power, performance, and area results and drive design optimizations to meet product objectives
  • Partner with synthesis, DFT, and physical design teams to resolve timing, power, and implementation challenges
  • Support PCB design and system integration activities to ensure successful product development and deployment

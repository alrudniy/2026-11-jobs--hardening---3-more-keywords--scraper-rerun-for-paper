Job description

Amentum is seeking a Radiation Effects Scientist to join our team in Indiana. The role focuses on assessing and mitigating the impact of radiation on system performance, including modeling interactions with electronic components and conducting tests across the product lifecycle.

Ideal candidates hold a Master's degree in Physics or a related field, 10+ years of radiation engineering experience, a...nd the ability to obtain a Secret US Government Clearance.

Qualifications
• Master's degree in Physics, Electrical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, or other relevant field.
• 10+ years of Radiation Engineering, electronic component testing, or related experience.
• Must be able to obtain and maintain a Secret US Government Clearance; US citizenship required.

Responsibilities
• Support engineering decisions based on radiation effects on overall system performance.
• Design and harden systems across multiple radiation effects (dose rate, total ionizing dose (TID), neutron displacement damage (NDD), and single event effects (SEE)).
• Work with interdisciplinary teams to ensure radiation hardening is maintained across the product lifecycle.
• Assist in test plan development and review.
• Review Reports.
• Participate in Radiation Review Boards.
• Help generate templates and guides.
• Assist in developing training materials and tools for new hires.
• Assist with testing efforts, test station setup and validation and verification efforts.
• Collaborate on program requirements and provide feedback on approach to meet requirements.

Job description

Welcome to Boeing, a global leader in aerospace technology and innovation. We are currently seeking a highly skilled and motivated Semiconductor Radiation Effects Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring the reliability and effectiveness of our semiconductor systems in the face of radiation exposure. We are looking for an individual with a strong background in ...semiconductor physics and radiation effects, who is passionate about pushing the boundaries of technology and delivering cutting-edge solutions. Join us and be a part of shaping the future of aerospace technology.

Conduct research and analysis on the effects of radiation on semiconductor systems, and use this information to develop and implement strategies for ensuring system reliability and effectiveness.

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand the specific requirements and challenges of various aerospace projects, and propose solutions that meet these needs.

Conduct testing and evaluation of semiconductor systems under simulated radiation conditions, and analyze and interpret the results to identify potential areas for improvement.

Develop and maintain testing protocols and procedures to ensure consistent and accurate evaluation of semiconductor systems.

Stay up-to-date with industry developments and advancements in semiconductor technology and radiation effects, and incorporate this knowledge into project work.

Utilize advanced analytical tools and techniques to model and predict the behavior of semiconductor systems under different radiation exposure scenarios.

Communicate technical information and findings in a clear and concise manner to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Work closely with suppliers and vendors to identify and evaluate new semiconductor materials and technologies that may improve system performance in radiation-prone environments.

Collaborate with other engineers and departments within the company to ensure the successful integration of semiconductor systems into overall aerospace products.

Actively participate in the development and implementation of company-wide quality control and risk management processes related to semiconductor radiation effects.

Mentor and provide technical guidance to more junior team members, and assist in their professional development.

Adhere to all safety protocols and procedures while working with potentially hazardous materials and equipment.

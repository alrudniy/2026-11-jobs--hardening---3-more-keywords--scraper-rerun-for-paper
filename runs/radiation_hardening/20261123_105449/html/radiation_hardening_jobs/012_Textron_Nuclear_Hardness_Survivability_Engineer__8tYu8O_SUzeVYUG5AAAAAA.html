Job description

Welcome to Textron, a leading global aerospace and defense company dedicated to providing innovative and reliable solutions for our customers. We are currently seeking a highly skilled Nuclear Hardness & Survivability Engineer III/IV to join our team. As a member of our dynamic and collaborative engineering department, you will play a critical role in ensuring the survivability and resilience of o...ur products in nuclear environments. If you are a self-motivated, detail-oriented individual with a passion for engineering and a strong background in nuclear hardening and survivability, we want to hear from you. Join us and be a part of shaping the future of our company and the defense industry.

Develop and implement strategies to ensure the survivability and resilience of Textron's products in nuclear environments.
Conduct thorough analyses and tests of product designs to identify potential vulnerabilities to nuclear threats.
Collaborate with other engineers and departments to integrate nuclear hardening and survivability measures into product designs.
Stay up-to-date on industry standards and regulations related to nuclear hardening and survivability.
Communicate with customers and stakeholders to understand their specific needs and requirements related to nuclear environments.
Utilize advanced engineering tools and software to design and simulate potential nuclear scenarios and their impact on products.
Provide technical support and guidance to other engineers and team members regarding nuclear hardening and survivability.
Conduct training and informational sessions for employees on best practices for nuclear hardening and survivability.
Participate in product development meetings and provide insights and recommendations based on your expertise in nuclear hardening and survivability.
Continuously evaluate and improve upon existing nuclear hardening and survivability processes and procedures within the company.

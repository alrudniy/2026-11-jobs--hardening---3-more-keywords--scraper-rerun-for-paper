Amentum is seeking a Radiation Effects Subject Matter Expert to assess and mitigate radiation impact on system performance across environments and components. Qualifications
• Bachelor’s degree in Physics, Electrical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering or other relevant field.
• 10+ years of Radiation Engineering or related experience.
• Must have or be able to obtain and maintain a US Government Secret Clearance (US Citizenship required).
• Preferred: Master’s degree or PhD; current Secret or Top-Secret DoD clearance.

Responsibilities
• Support engineering decisions based on radiation effects on overall system performance.
• Design and harden systems across multiple radiation effects (dose rate, TID, NDD, SEE).
• Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to maintain radiation hardening across the product lifecycle.
• Assist in test plan development and review; review reports; participate in Radiation Review Boards.
• Help generate templates and guides; develop training materials and tools for new hires.
• Assist with testing, test station setup, validation and verification; provide feedback on program requirements.
